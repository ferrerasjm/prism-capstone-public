# Risk review and support actions

1. A Discipline Officer reviews a student's current score and factors. A new action requires a High or Critical score.
2. Select an action type, enter a plan of 20?5,000 characters, and choose a follow-up date of today or later.
3. The action starts In Progress and is assigned to its creating officer. Only one open action of each type is allowed per student.
4. Reschedule an open action or close it as Completed/Cancelled with an outcome or reason of 20?2,000 characters.
5. Closed actions cannot be edited or reopened through this workflow.

Types include risk review, support meeting, monitoring, referral for support services, compliance follow-up, and other support action. Recording an action does not create a formal guidance referral or sanction.

Overdue and Needs Review are dashboard conditions. Later score changes do not rewrite the original action context or automatically close it. Creation and updates are audited.

See [risk scoring](RISK_SCORING_METHODOLOGY.md) and [RiskInterventionService](../app/Services/RiskInterventionService.php).
